## Unleash Limitless Creativity with DEX 3 & SoundCloud GO Combo – Over 200M Tracks at Your Fingertips

[![Access 200+ Million Songs with DEX 3 x SoundCloud](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/soundcloud-go-dex3-cover.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/soundcloud-go-dex3-cover.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Access 200+ Million Songs with DEX 3 x SoundCloud")

**DJ Software creators Digital 1 Audio (PCDJ) have released DEX 3.13 with in-app support for the SoundCloud Go+ subscription service, providing DJs with the ability to access and mix over 200 million songs for a low monthly fee.**

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![DEX 3 x SoundCloud Go+ Streaming](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/dex3xsoundcloud-screenshot1920x1080.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "DEX 3 x SoundCloud Go+ Streaming")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/dex3xsoundcloud-screenshot1920x1080.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1)

Clearwater, Florida, 4/30/19 Digital 1 Audio, Inc ([http://www.pcdj.com](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)), pioneers of DJ software products designed for professional DJs, launched version 3.13 of their [DEX 3 DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) (Mac/Windows) complete with SoundCloud Go+ subscription integration. SoundCloud Go+ provides DEX3 DJs with in-app access to SoundCloud’s full catalog of 200+ million premium and underground tracks. DJs can use SoundCloud to discover new music and build playlists that synchronize with DEX3’s browser automatically. The tight-nit integration means lightening quick track buffering and trouble-free streaming.

_“Adding in-app support for the SoundCloud Go+ subscription to DEX 3 furthers our goal in creating a true end-to-end DJ software solution for the today’s versatile DJ.”_ says Digital 1 Audio’s General Manager Ryan Sherr. _“DEX 3 allows DJs to intuitively mix music, music videos, and host karaoke. We’ve supported Party Tyme’s karaoke’s subscription in DEX 3 since 2018, adding SoundCloud Go+ support means customers no longer need to find their music elsewhere – they can create an account through DEX 3, log in, and instantly stream from a massive catalog of high-quality tracks.”_

**SoundCloud Go+ is $9.99/m (or $4.99/m with a Pro Unlimited subscription) after 1-month free trial. What subscribers get with SoundCloud Go+:**

* Access full catalog, no previews
* Ad-free listening
* High quality audio

**Here are notable features and/or improvements included in DEX 3 version 3.13:**

* SoundCloud Go+ subscription integration (streaming)
* Better compatibility with macOS Mojave (including Dark Mode)
* New controllers supported: Denon DN-MCX8000 and Reloop TOUCH (over 100 DJ controllers supported)
* Improved support for Party Tyme Karaoke (karaoke subscription) and Karaoke.net
* Fixed Retina mode on MacBookPro 15″ 2016 and newer
* Various Performance improvements

**DEX 3.13’s manufacturer’s suggested retail price is $179\.**

**Recommended System Requirements:**

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS: WINDOWS

* Windows: 7 / Windows 8 / Windows 8.1 / Windows 10
* Intel Core i3/i5/i7
* 4 GIG RAM or Better
* Video Card: Dedicated Graphics Card with at least 512MB (or integrated Intel HD 4000 Series or better)
* DirectX/ASIO Compatible Multi-Channel Audio Interface (and/or USB audio interface)
* 200 MB free on the hard-drive

**SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS: MAC**

* Mac: OSX 10.9 or greater
* Intel Core i3/i5/i7
* 4 GIG RAM or Better
* Video Card: Dedicated Graphics Card with at least 512MB (or Intel HD 4000/Iris Series or better)
* Multi-channel Core Audio soundcard (and/or USB audio interface)
* 200 MB free on the hard-drive

**Important Note: The above are recommendations for standard 480p (SD) Video Mixing:**

* For 720p HD videos: At least 4GB of RAM, 8GB recommended, a video card with at least 512MB dedicated video RAM
* For 1080p/1080i full HD videos: At least 4GB of RAM, 8GB recommended, a video card with at least 1GB dedicated video RAM
