The subscription service is the only professional platform for pro KJs and venues, and subscribers can use the in-app subscription legally for commercial performances.

PCDJ Karaoki is [karaoke software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) designed for Pro KJs that demand the best singer rotation management. With Karaoke Cloud Pro directly integrated it’s the total karaoke hosting solution. Just sign in through PCDJ Karaoki and you’ll have immediate access to around 10,000 high-quality karaoke songs from some of the best brands in the business. New karaoke tracks are added all the time at no additional charge for subscribers, and since you download the files to your local hard drive you only have to sync with the online server once a month to verify you’re still paying. This means you can use [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) with the [Karaoke Cloud Pro](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) service offline, _without an internet connection_.
