## Download Your Pioneer DJ SX2 Mapping Software for Deckadance Version 3 - Professional Controller Tutorials

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/ddjsx3-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/ddjsx3-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "ddjsx3-coverimage")

The **Pioneer DDJ-SX2** Map for DEX 3 is now available for download.

![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/ddj-sx-2-angle.jpg?fit=300%2C200&ssl=1 "ddj-sx-2-angle")

The original DDJ-SX controller from Pioneer DJ has long been one of the most popular [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) among DEX 3 users. It’s 4-deck design lends itself beautifully to DEX 3’s feature-set, whether mixing audio tracks or video mixing. Pioneer DJ’s low-latency ASIO/Core Audio interface has been one of the best on the market and the large pads are perfect for setting and triggering hot cues or stored loops.

[LARGER IMAGE ![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/ddj-sx2-front-rear.jpg?fit=300%2C200&ssl=1 "ddj-sx2-front-rear")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/ddj-sx2-front-rear.jpg?fit=580%2C387&ssl=1)

The Pioneer DDJ-SX2 improves on the original DDJ-SX design with RGB backlit pads, improved jog wheels, DVS (digital vinyl system) support and overall improvements to a bunch of other bits. It’s easily been the DJ controller DEX 3 DJ software users have been requesting support for the most. Now DEX 3 users have the same plug-and-play native support they had with the original DDJ-SX with the revamped DDJ-SX2.

The DDJ-SX2 is listed at $999 on most online DJ equipment retailers.
